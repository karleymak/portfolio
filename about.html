I am a graphic designer based in the Greater Sacramento area. I specialize in communication, marketing, and building intuitive, interactive experiences.

I care a lot about the work I do and the places or people it may travel to. I aim to build and design experiences surrounding themes of community and caring about matters larger than ourselves. I am inspired by anything and everything!

When I'm not designing, you can find me perusing antique stores, falling down Wikipedia rabbit holes, or taking my sketchbook to the lake.

I enjoy music from the 70s, self-serve fro-yo shops, and occasionally playing tennis.
